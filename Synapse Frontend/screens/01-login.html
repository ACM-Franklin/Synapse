Let people in through Discord, then get the hell out of the way.

Synapse does not need a theatrical onboarding funnel. The backend owns the OAuth dance, sets state, handles the callback, and decides whether the user belongs here. The frontend should present a clean entry point, a calm explanation of what access means, and a blunt denial state when the user is not part of the guild or lacks the right role.
