Screen 06 · Leaderboard

Show the ranking, show the score movement, and skip the tacky public spectacle.

The leaderboard matters because community systems need visible feedback loops. It does not need to become a public marketing page or a fake esports control room. For MVP, keep it authenticated, scoped to the guild, and tied to real reward data the member can inspect elsewhere.

Current boundary

The backend already supports authenticated leaderboard reads. Season management, public sharing, and operator tooling belong later or not at all.
